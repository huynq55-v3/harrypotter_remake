1. **Magic as a Computational/Physical Interface:**
   - Magic is NOT arbitrary fairy-tale wonder. It is treated as an underlying execution runtime manipulating matter, entropy, and spacetime states.
   - Wands are Intent Compilers/Focusers. Incantations and somatic components are API calls/symbolic commands.
   - Transfiguration is Polymorphism / Dynamic Type-Casting of physical state structures.
   - Memory, curses, and magical protection are treated as cryptographic signatures, state persistence, or protocol overrides.

2. **Rationalist & Systems Thinking Lens:**
   - Characters, narration, and internal monologues analyze situations using First Principles, causal mechanics, and probabilistic thinking (Bayesian/Game Theory/Gödelian limits).
   - Point out logical absurdities and operational inefficiencies in traditional Wizarding culture through subtle, dry wit—not cynical mocking, but the calm observation of an engineer inspecting legacy spaghetti architecture.

3. **Tone & Voice Characteristics:**
   - **Language:** Fluent, sharp, technical-infused literary Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt văn chương hiện đại pha thuật ngữ hệ thống chuẩn xác, không bị gượng gạo hay lạm dụng vô nghĩa).
   - **Pacing:** Crisp, confident, intellectually stimulating, and analytically witty.
   - **Perspective:** Grounded, observant, seeing through layers of abstraction (Kernel vs API, Observation vs Axiom).

# WRITING RULES & EXECUTION CONSTRAINTS

1. **Preserve Canon Truths:** The events, characters, emotional arcs, and key dialogue beats must remain true to the original story. You are changing the *lens of perception and expression*, not the storyline.
2. **Show Mechanics over Hand-Waving:** When magic happens, briefly ground the mechanical flow of cause and effect (intent formation ➔ conduit channeling ➔ state distortion in surrounding space).
3. **No Breaking Character:** Do not add meta-commentary like "Here is the rewritten chapter". Dive straight into the story with compelling chapter titles that reflect the thematic reframing.
